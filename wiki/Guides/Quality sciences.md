Any quality science above rare is unviable for UPS.

Quality sciences achieve quality by producing quality inputs at the lowest level that are then processed "normally".

In order to be competitive, quality sciences should make used of advanced voiding techniques, especially train voiding.

If uncommon science is produced, it is beneficial to siphon off the Q3 rare materials as well and produce Q3 science with those.



Using quality science is beneficial for UPS at the labs: less inserters swings, less lab time.

Current results (before savings on the hub and transport lanes):

Red science: Q2 on par with Q1, Mixed ahead, transport lane/hub savings actually relevant and tip it in favor of red science.

Green science: Q2 behind, Mixed slightly ahead/even, transport lane/hub savings should tip in favor of Mixed.

Blue: Q2 far ahead, Mixed even more.

Yellow: Q2 ahead, Mixed even more.

Purple/Military: Quality science is not an option. 1) On patch builds are extremely strong 2) Grenades, walls, ammo, furnaces and rails don't allow for productivity in themselves and the production chains also have little productivity and 3) those items at the same time consume vast amounts of raw materials, especially stone and coal which do benefit only very little from productivity.

Space Science: Unclear, not benched yet. As production of space science is very cheap, likely Q2/Q3 mixed ahead if also taking into account the hub logistics. Looking at production only, Q1 probably ahead.

Gleba: Quality is not an option as there is no feasible method to produce quality raw materials

Vulcanus: On Patch mining Q2 slightly ahead, Mixed clearly ahead. Can use lava or train voiding.

Fulgora: Without train voiding, anything but Q1 is not an option. With train voiding, Q2 and Mixed come out clearly ahead. Very complex builds but huge gains are possible.

Aquilo: Mixed is ahead. Likely best to siphon off q3 ice from Fulgora and produce additional Q3 science in this amount.

Prom: Quality not an option



Summary: Mixed quality Q2+Q3 is best if going for quality, Q4/Q5 amounts are too small to be beneficial. Train voiding is very beneficial.

Mixed quality sciences: RGBY, Vulcanus, Fulgora, Aquilo, Space Science (?)

Common quality sciences: Military, Production, Gleba, Promethium
